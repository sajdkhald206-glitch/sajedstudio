What is the ATS format?

ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is the software most companies use to scan CVs before a recruiter opens them. It reads plain text and looks for standard headings and the keywords of the job. Columns, tables, icons and text inside images can make it skip your details, so a strong CV can be rejected without anyone reading it.

  • One column and standard headings: Summary, Experience, Skills, Education
  • Real text, no tables, icons or images
  • Job keywords and clear dates for every role
